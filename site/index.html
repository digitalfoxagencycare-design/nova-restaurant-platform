What you get

Everything a restaurant needs to take orders and run the day, in one place.

  • Your own ordering website and Android app

    Your name, your logo, your colours. Customers order from your link or your app, not from a marketplace.

  • Sign-in by WhatsApp code

    No passwords to remember. A customer enters their mobile number and gets a code on WhatsApp.

  • Order updates on WhatsApp

    Placed, being prepared, ready, on the way and delivered. All sent from one Nova number, so you do not run any messaging yourself.

  • Online payment into your own account

    Customers pay through Razorpay and the money goes straight to your Razorpay account. Nova never holds your money. Cash on delivery works too.

  • Delivery partner app

    Your riders get their own app. The customer gives a delivery code at the door, so an order is only marked delivered when it really is.

  • Owner app

    See today's orders and sales on your phone, update order status, pause ordering when the kitchen is full, and manage offers.

  • Counter billing with thermal printing

    Bills, split payments, kitchen tickets and refunds with manager approval. Prints on a TVS thermal printer through a small program on the counter computer.

  • Kitchen screen

    Counter, online and delivery orders show up on one kitchen screen, in the order they came in.

  • Offers and customers

    Create coupon offers and see who your customers are and what they order, in your own list.

  • Many restaurants, one team

    Nova looks after every restaurant from one control console: set-up, brand, health checks and data export when you need it.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

We discuss fees on a call, once we understand your restaurant. There is nothing to guess from this page. Use "Talk to us" and we will explain it plainly.

Who owns my customer data?

You do. Your customers, orders and menu belong to your restaurant. They are kept separately from every other restaurant on Nova.

Can we keep our own number and brand?

Your brand, yes: your name, logo and colours are what customers see. Order messages on WhatsApp are sent from one shared Nova number today, not from your own number.

Where does the money from online payments go?

Straight into your own Razorpay account. Nova does not hold or touch it. Razorpay's own charges apply as per your agreement with them.

What if the internet is down?

Counter billing needs internet today. If it is down, the counter cannot bill until it is back. Plan for a backup, such as a mobile hotspot.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Nova can export your restaurant's data when you ask. It does not include passwords, PINs or payment keys.
