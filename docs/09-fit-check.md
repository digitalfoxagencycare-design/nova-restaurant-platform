# 9. Who fits this platform — and a questionnaire

## 9.1 Fit by business type
| Business | Fit | What differs from the source |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant / food court | **Native** | — |
| Cloud kitchen / delivery-only | **Native** | Hide dine-in & tables; aggregator inbox matters |
| Café / tea stall / bakery / sweet shop | **Native** | Counter-heavy; faster POS; pre-orders |
| Tiffin / mess / meal subscriptions | High | **Subscriptions** (weekly/monthly plans, pause days) — new module |
| Meat / fish / vegetable shop | High | Weight-based pricing (per kg), cut/clean options, stock |
| Catering | Medium | Quotes, advance payments, event dates |
| Salons, clinics (appointments) | Medium | Replace tables with **slots/resources**; menu → services |
| Wholesale / fireworks / nursery | Partial | Catalog + orders + billing fit; delivery radius, GST invoicing, credit ledgers differ |

Rule of thumb: if the business **sells a catalog, takes orders, collects payment, fulfils at a place or address, and wants repeat customers**, it fits. If it needs **inventory with batches, B2B credit terms or appointments**, add the matching module first.

## 9.2 Onboarding questionnaire (collect before creating a tenant)
**Business** — legal name, brand name, GSTIN, FSSAI (food), address(es), hours, languages, support contacts.
**Ordering** — dine-in / takeaway / delivery? table service? pre-order? minimum order? packaging charge?
**Delivery** — own riders or partner? radius or zones? fee rules? free-delivery threshold? COD allowed (hours)?
**Money** — tax mode and rates; payment methods; Razorpay account (own or Nova-collected); refunds policy; tips.
**Menu** — source (sheet/POS export/photos); categories; variants; veg/non-veg; stations; images available?
**Loyalty & offers** — points rules; welcome offer; coupon types wanted; referral?
**Staff** — roles and who needs which permissions; devices (POS PC, printer model, KDS tablet).
**Channels** — website domain; WhatsApp number (own WABA?); Instagram; Google Business; Meta/Google ads accounts for the console.
**Brand** — logo files, colours, fonts, photos, tone of voice, app name/icon for white-label.
**Legal** — privacy policy, terms, refund/cancellation text approved by the owner.

## 9.3 Definition of "onboarded"
Menu live · one test order paid and printed · kitchen screen receives it · delivery assigned (if used) · WhatsApp message received · owner sees the sale in the console · rollback plan written.
